# eDataPay GitHub Development

eDataPay GitHub Development is the central development environment for our ideas, applications, APIs, and technology solutions serving merchants, business owners, ISOs, PSPs, fintech companies, payment gateways, acquirers, and banking partners.

Our goal is to develop practical tools that help businesses:

* Accept and manage payments
* Acquire and retain customers
* Manage risk and underwriting
* Connect commerce channels
* Automate business operations
* Use data and AI to make better business decisions

## Our Experience

Founded and directed by **Adam Elisha**, who has worked in the payments and merchant-services industry since **1998**, our development is grounded in decades of hands-on experience with payment technologies, bankcard management platforms, payment gateways, merchant applications, business software, integrations, and financial technology.

We combine real-world payment-industry experience with modern:

* APIs and integrations
* Cloud infrastructure
* Automation
* AI-assisted development
* Financial technology
